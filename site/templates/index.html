Good to know

The questions people ask before they install it.

Does it change ZimaOS or CasaOS?
No system files change. On ZimaOS IceDash is a module beside the stock dashboard. On CasaOS it takes the main address and keeps the stock UI at /casaos/; the uninstall script gives the address back. Apps installed from IceDash are normal apps of your system.
Where do I open it?
On ZimaOS, open the IceDash tile or http://<your-server>/modules/icedash/index.html. An admin can make it open at the server's address in Settings → General, and the ZimaOS dashboard then stays at /index.html. On CasaOS it's at http://<your-server>/, with the stock UI at /casaos/. Sign in with your ZimaOS or CasaOS account.
How do I uninstall it?

Over SSH on your server, on ZimaOS or CasaOS:

curl -fsSL {{base_url}}uninstall.sh | sudo sh

It removes IceDash and gives your server's address back to the stock ZimaOS or CasaOS dashboard. Your IceDash settings and notifications stay in /DATA/.icedash, in case you install it again. To delete those too:

curl -fsSL {{base_url}}uninstall.sh | sudo sh -s -- --purge

Apps you installed from IceDash stay either way.

How does it update?
IceDash checks for new versions and shows Update now in its menu. If an update fails to start, the previous version comes back by itself. An admin can also let it install updates at night, between 02:00 and 05:00.
Who can do what?
You sign in with your ZimaOS or CasaOS account. Admins get everything. Members get their own look, dock and notifications and can open apps, but not Files, the Terminal, credentials or app actions.
Are mods safe?
Themes are colours and CSS only. Each widget runs in its own sandbox, never sees your sign-in, and shows the permissions it asks for before you turn it on.
Is it free?
Yes. IceDash is free and open source, and so is IceStore, the app store inside it.
